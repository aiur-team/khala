## Outcome and acceptance

An installable skill that any agent can add: it starts a listener with a durable cursor, reconnect and duplicate suppression, and provides the send command. It states its own permission cost rather than hiding it.

## Amends or supersedes

New surface, required by P15 and by P01 support-any-model. It is the generic path for harnesses with no proven native route, and it is what KHA-153 selects when no adapter reports a usable route. It closes the four durability gaps `docs/evidence/claude.md` recorded against the `Monitor` route.

Real proof belongs to the named integration/evidence owner in the repository layout; component tests are not deferred until then. A capability record may claim `support: "tested"` only when its `evidenceRef` names a document that exists under `docs/evidence/`.
